Usucha, one bowl

matcha
2 g
water
80°C · 70 ml
whisk
20 sec

You will need a bowl (chawan), a bamboo whisk (chasen), a scoop (chashaku) and a small sieve.

  1. 1

    Add the matcha

    Sift 2 g of matcha into a warm bowl.

  2. 2

    Add hot water

    Pour in 70 ml of water at 80°C. Hot, not boiling.

  3. 3

    Whisk

    Whisk in a zigzag, not circles, until a fine foam forms.

  4. 4

    Make it a latte

    Optional: pour it over cold or steamed milk.

  5. 5

    Share it

    Snap a photo and tag us. We love seeing your bowl.
